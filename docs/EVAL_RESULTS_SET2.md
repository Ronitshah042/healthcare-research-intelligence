## Known weaknesses

- The starter questions were written by the developer from the stored abstracts, so their wording favours retrieval; real users' questions will score lower.
- Relevant articles were labelled by the developer; entries marked `needs_review` are unconfirmed.
- The judge (Groq) is a different model from the answer model (Gemini), but AI judges can still be lenient; the spot-check sheet measures its agreement with a human.
- Evidence is chosen per passage: one article can fill most of the passages sent to the model, crowding out other relevant articles.
- Passages longer than the embedding model's 512-token limit are cut off before embedding (`hri embed-passages` reports how many).
- Citation validity undercounts valid citations in answers withheld for having too many invented ones (their text is replaced).
- Retrieval is scored per article; a relevant article retrieved through an unhelpful passage still counts as found.
